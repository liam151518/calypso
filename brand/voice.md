## Voice principles

**Write like a gacha addict, not a marketer.**

Three rules:

1. **Use "you" and "I" — never "we" and "our users".**
2. **Show, don't sell.** Describe the toy, the reveal, the cabinet. Don't pitch.
3. **Treat the reader as someone who's already hooked.** They know what a capsule is. They know what a tier list is. Don't explain gacha to a gacha player.

## Tone examples

### 1. "Pull reaction" — sharing the moment of reveal

> just pulled a pink-cabinet Damascus figure I didn't even know was in this drop. sitting on my desk for the next 6 hours minimum.

**Pattern:** past tense, sensory detail, specific cabinet color, mild obsession energy, no marketing.

### 2. "Cabinet hype" — announcing a new cabinet

> Orange cabinet restocked at the Rosebank machine. someone please go pull before I burn my wallet down again. (I will not.)

**Pattern:** direct address, second-person bait, self-aware about the addiction, light emoji-or-symbol restraint.

### 3. "Tier list" — sharing strategy

> New tier list up on the site. S-tier is honestly fewer figures than I expected — the Damascus set carries harder than people think.

**Pattern:** opinionated, casual, links to site subtly ("on the site"), uses domain vocabulary.

### 4. "Set completion" — celebrating the binder

> 14/16 on the Spring drop. two more and I can stop refreshing at 2am. (I cannot.)

**Pattern:** numeric, progress-oriented, self-deprecating, parentheses for the honest bit.

### 5. "IRL cabinet" — physical location content

> Checked in at the Rosebank cabinet today. free spin for the QR check-in. pulled a yellow-tier figure that I would've skipped online. sometimes the floor knows.

**Pattern:** first-person, specific location, ties to a real feature (QR check-in), implies the offline experience is special.

## Banned words / framing

From `../Gacha Luka/docs/GATCHA_KINGDOM_PRODUCT_BRIEF.md` section 3 (Legal positioning):

- **NEVER use:** "bet", "wager", "odds", "winning", "jackpot", "chance to win", "prize", "lucky draw", "cash", "money payout"
- **AVOID framing:** "you could win", "win big", "risk-free", "guaranteed win"
- **PREFER:** "spin", "pull", "open", "reveal", "collect", "complete the set", "blind box", "mystery capsule", "random figure"

## Hashtag strategy

- **3-5 hashtags per post**, mix broad + niche
- **Broad:** `#gacha` `#capsuletoy` `#blindbox` `#collectibles`
- **Niche:** `#GatchaKingdom` `#gachakingdoms` `#capsetoys` `#southafrica` `#johannesburg`
- **Never** use `#gambling` or anything that implies wagering

---

## Emoji discipline

- **1-3 emojis max per caption**
- Preferred: ✨ 🎴 🐱 🎉 (matches the kawaii/arcade vibe)
- Banned: 💰 💸 🎰 🎲 (gambling energy)
- Use sparingly — emoji spam is the #1 sign of low-quality ad copy

## Call-to-action patterns

**Subtle (preferred):**
- "full tier list on gachakingdoms.com"
- "see the cabinet map"
- "check the binder"
- "pull list updated"

**Direct (use sparingly):**
- "tap the link in bio"
- "spin now → gachakingdoms.com/spin"
- "start your collection → gachakingdoms.com"

**Banned:**
- "CLICK HERE NOW"
- "LIMITED TIME OFFER"
- "DON'T MISS OUT"
- "EXCLUSIVE DEAL"
- "ACT FAST"

## Regional flavor

South African voice. Casual, warm, direct. Self-deprecating humor lands. Don't try to sound American or British. Don't use "y'all" or "innit."

References that land: Rosebank, Johannesburg, online vs IRL, queue at the cabinet.
