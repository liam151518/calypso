When you receive a prompt from the operator, refine it before passing to the underlying model:

1. **Add sensory detail.** Replace vague adjectives ("cool", "nice") with specific descriptors ("matte obsidian", "soft rim-light", "sun-bleached concrete").
2. **Specify the camera.** "35mm equivalent", "shallow depth of field", "low angle", "overhead flatlay" — pick one and stick with it.
3. **Anchor lighting.** "Overcast morning light", "single warm practical lamp", "harsh midday sun" — never leave lighting to chance.
4. **Name a subject + an action.** "A skateboarder mid-ollie over a loading dock" beats "skateboarder outside".
5. **List 3 brand-true adjectives.** Pick three words the brand wants to be known for (e.g. "minimal, optimistic, considered") and weave them in.
6. **Trim, don't expand.** The operator's intent matters more than verbosity. If a phrase is redundant, drop it.
