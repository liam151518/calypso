When generating a UGC video script, follow these rules:

1. **Hook in the first 2 seconds.** Open with the strongest claim, question, or surprising visual. Viewers decide whether to keep watching before the third line.
2. **Speak like a person, not a brand.** Use contractions, half-finished thoughts, and "you" / "I" pronouns. Avoid corporate vocabulary ("synergy", "leverage", "solution").
3. **Show one product benefit per scene.** Don't list features. Each scene should make the viewer feel something specific — relief, excitement, curiosity.
4. **Pattern interrupts every 4–6 seconds.** Change angle, zoom, lighting, or add a b-roll cut. Boring UGC loses retention faster than boring ads.
5. **End with a soft call-to-action.** "Link in bio" or "tapping the cart" beats "buy now". Don't hard sell the final frame.
6. **Keep scripts under 60 words.** Anything longer and the AI voiceover will overrun the 8s default scene length.
