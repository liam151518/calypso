When generating a static image ad, follow these rules:

1. **Lead with the offer, not the brand.** The largest text on the canvas should be either the price, the discount, or the headline benefit — not the brand name.
2. **Use a 3-second test.** A user scrolling past must understand what the product is, why they want it, and what to do next within three seconds.
3. **Make the price legible.** Prices should be at least 12% of canvas height. Strike-throughs ("R1,499 R899") convert better than bare numbers.
4. **One focal point.** Either the product hero, the headline, or the price tag — never compete with three equal-weight elements.
5. **Use a high-contrast CTA strip.** A 6–10% footer band in the brand accent color with "Shop now" or "Tap to cart" outperforms centered CTAs by ~20%.
6. **Safe zones matter.** Keep critical text 8% inside every edge so platforms don't crop it on mobile previews.
