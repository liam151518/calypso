## Context

The video pipeline uses MiniMax H3 as its primary video generation model. H3 is released under the **MiniMax H3 Community License Agreement** (bundled with the open-source release on Hugging Face).

We are using H3-generated video in **paid social media ads** on X, Instagram, and TikTok. We need to know whether the license permits this.

## The four questions to answer

### 1. Can we use H3-generated video in paid social media ads?

**To be filled in by Adam.** Look for the commercial-use clause.

Common patterns:
- Permits non-commercial use freely
- Permits commercial use up to a revenue threshold (e.g., $1M ARR)
- Permits commercial use with attribution requirement
- Prohibits commercial use entirely (rare for community licenses)
